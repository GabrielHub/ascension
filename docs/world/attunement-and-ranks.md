# Attunement And Ranks

Some people have abilities that exceed normal human limits. In the world of Ascension, these abilities are called **attunements**. Boss does not have one.

## How Attunements Work

- Attunements are innate. You either have one or you do not. They cannot be learned, bought, or transferred.
- Attunements are permanent and fixed. The abilities an operator has when they walk through the door are the abilities they will always have. Training can improve conditioning and discipline, but it cannot change what an attunement fundamentally is.
- Attunements manifest along a spectrum of intensity. This is what rank measures. Most attuned people are F or E rank - modestly superhuman, useful in low-threat dungeons but not special. A few are exceptional. S-rank operators are celebrities.
- Attunements are not magic in the traditional sense. There are no spells, no mana bars, no incantations. An attuned person's ability is like an extension of their body - something they do, not something they cast.
- Attunements have limits. Even an S-rank operator gets tired, makes mistakes, and can be killed by something they did not see coming. Rank is not invincibility.

## Rank And Attunement

Rank measures the innate ceiling of an operator's attunement. It is extremely difficult to change.

| Rank | Rarity         | Description                                                                                                           |
| ---- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| F    | Very common    | Barely superhuman. Can handle the lowest-threat dungeons. The bulk of the early labor market.                         |
| E    | Common         | Noticeably above human limits. Reliable for routine clearance work.                                                   |
| D    | Uncommon       | Genuinely dangerous. Can handle mid-tier dungeons. Starting to get expensive to retain.                               |
| C    | Rare           | Seriously powerful. High-tier dungeon capable. Other guilds want them.                                                |
| B    | Very rare      | Elite. Can anchor a raid team against major threats. Retention is a real problem.                                     |
| A    | Extremely rare | Exceptional. Near the top of human attunement. Known in the industry by name.                                         |
| S    | Legendary      | Celebrities. Unique visual presence, unique gear, massive resource investment to recruit and retain. Endgame content. |

**Rank advancement is possible but extremely rare.** Most operators live and die at the rank they started with.

## Public Perception

- Attuned people are known to exist. This is not a secret. But you cannot tell by looking - attunement is invisible until demonstrated.
- Public opinion is mixed. High-rank operators are treated like athletes or minor celebrities. Low-rank operators are treated like anyone else with a blue-collar hazard job.
- S-rank operators are famous. They have endorsement deals, fan followings, and opinions about which guilds are "beneath them."
- No attunement types are currently stigmatized or legally restricted. The regulatory framework is still too young and too confused to have gotten that specific yet. This may change as the world develops.
- Boss is not attuned, and since attunement is invisible, this only comes up when people ask why Boss does not go on raids. The answer is always some variation of "I'm management."
